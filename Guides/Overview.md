# Forgebase Overview

Forgebase is a **docs-first backend foundation** designed for students, indie builders, and early-stage startups who want to build serious systems without getting lost in backend complexity.

Forgebase is not a framework.  
It is a **set of proven backend patterns, architectural decisions, and starter modules** that can be adapted to different products.

---

## What Forgebase Solves

- Backend complexity for early builders
- Unclear architecture decisions
- Overengineering before product validation
- Insecure or poorly designed auth & data models

Forgebase focuses on **clarity, safety, and extensibility**.

---

## Core Pieces

- **Architecture**: clearly separated responsibilities
- **Backend patterns**: APIs, auth, data flow, realtime
- **Security model**: safe-by-default decisions
- **Guides**: how to build, customize, and operate
- **Validation**: real-world usage via EduBro

---

## How Everything Connects

Forgebase documents *why* and *how*.  
Your product decides *what* to build.

---

## What Forgebase Is NOT

- Not a full backend-as-a-service
- Not tied to a single tech stack
- Not a one-size-fits-all solution

It is **opinionated, not restrictive**.
