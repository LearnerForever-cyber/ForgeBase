# Tradeoffs

Every system makes tradeoffs.

Forgebase documents them explicitly.

---

## What You Gain

By using Forgebase, you gain:
- Clear backend structure
- Safer authentication patterns
- Predictable data access
- Easier onboarding for teammates
- Confidence when adding features

---

## What You Give Up

You give up:
- Total freedom in architecture
- One-click abstractions
- Rapid experimentation without structure

Forgebase requires intentional decisions.

---

## Speed vs Stability

Forgebase may feel slower at first.

This is expected.

However:
- Refactors are fewer
- Bugs are easier to trace
- Scaling decisions are clearer

The time saved later outweighs the time spent early.

---

## Learning vs Convenience

Forgebase prioritizes learning through building.

This means:
- You will see more internals
- You will read more explanations
- You will understand more deeply

Convenience is secondary.

---

## Who Should Reconsider Using Forgebase

Forgebase may not be a good fit if:
- You want instant results without learning
- You dislike opinionated systems
- Your project is short-lived or experimental

---

## Who Benefits the Most

Forgebase works best for:
- Students building long-term projects
- Founders validating serious ideas
- Teams that value clarity and safety

---

Tradeoffs are not flaws.

They are **design choices**.
